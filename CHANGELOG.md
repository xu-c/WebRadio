## 1.0.12-1

### 简体中文

- 新增轻量操作音效，提供导航、确认和返回提示。
- 帮助页按 4 开关音效，设置在重启后保留。
- 代码以 MIT 协议开源，安装包附带许可证。

### English

- Added short navigation, confirmation, and back sounds.
- Press 4 on the Controls screen to toggle sounds. The preference survives restarts.
- Released the source code under MIT and included the license in the package.

## 1.0.11-1

### 简体中文

- 主界面右上角显示剩余电量，电量低时用红色提示。
- 音量页同时显示应用音量和 Zero 当前音频输出的系统音量。
- 用上下键或 F / X 选择要调的音量，用左右键或 Z / C 调整。数字快捷键 4 / 5 直接调应用音量，6 / 7 直接调系统音量。

### English

- The main screen shows the battery percentage in the top-right corner and highlights low charge in red.
- The volume screen shows both WebRadio volume and system volume for the active audio output.
- Use Up / Down or F / X to select a volume, then Left / Right or Z / C to adjust it. Use 4 / 5 for app volume and 6 / 7 for system volume directly.

## 1.0.9-1

### 简体中文

- 重做 320×170 界面，突出电台列表、播放状态和音量。播放栏可显示电台名及电台流提供的节目或曲名。
- 新增全拼搜索，例如输入 `zhongguozhisheng` 查找「中国之声」。原有台名搜索可继续输入英文、频率或呼号。搜索默认沿用筛选条件，可切换到全球。
- 搜索超过 10 秒后显示耗时和取消提示。短按 Esc 或按 8 可从搜索返回原列表和选中位置，长按 Esc 3 秒退出。
- 按 Q 一键继续收听上次成功播放的电台。电台详情显示国家／地区、语言和类型。

### English

- Redesigned the 320×170 interface with clearer lists, playback status, and volume. The player shows the station and any program or track title supplied by the stream.
- Added full-pinyin search for Chinese station names. Standard search still accepts English names, frequencies, and call signs. Search uses the current filters by default, with a global option.
- Searches taking 10 seconds show elapsed time and an Esc cancel hint. Tap Esc or press 8 to return to the previous list and selection. Hold Esc for three seconds to quit.
- Press Q to resume the last station that played successfully. Station details show country or region, language, and genres.
